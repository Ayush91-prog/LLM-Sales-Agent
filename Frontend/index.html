How the Autonomous Sales Agent Works

1. Intent & Multi-Store Resolution

Customers ask for products naturally (e.g. "I need a Kg Toy Dog" or "Find me a Samsung S24"). The AI agent searches the unified multi-tenant database, identifies which merchant stocks the item, and attributes pricing without requiring the shopper to manually pick a store.

2. Policy-Bound Negotiation

Merchants define strict margin rules (e.g. max 10% discount, minimum order value, free shipping thresholds). When customers negotiate, the AI uses autonomous tool-calling to calculate and grant discounts strictly within the seller's approved limits.

3. Zero-Friction Fulfilment

Customer delivery addresses are bound upon sign-in. Orders are finalized with one confirmation, creating real-time ACID-compliant transactions in Neon PostgreSQL and instantly reflect on the merchant's fulfillment dashboard.

Platform Overview

It is designed for online retail customers seeking a personal conversational shopping assistant, and independent business owners who want an automated 24/7 sales representative that negotiates and closes sales without human staff.

The core software is open-source and free to explore for research, portfolio demonstration, and merchant prototyping. Live deployments run on cost-effective serverless architecture.
